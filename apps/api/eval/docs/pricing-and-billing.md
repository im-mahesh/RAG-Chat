# Pricing and Billing FAQ

Answers to the most common questions from customers about plans, invoices and refunds.

## Plans

Acme offers three plans. Free is for individuals and small experiments and costs nothing. Pro costs $49 per seat per month and adds priority support and higher usage limits. Enterprise is priced individually and adds single sign-on, an uptime service level agreement and a named account manager.

## Billing cycle

Monthly plans are billed on the same day each month as the original sign-up. Annual billing is available for Pro and gives two months free compared with paying monthly. Seats added in the middle of a billing period are charged pro rata for the remaining days.

## Invoices and payment terms

Invoices are emailed to the billing contact on the day they are issued. Card payments are collected immediately. Enterprise customers paying by bank transfer have net 30 payment terms, meaning payment is due 30 days after the invoice date. Late payments may lead to the account being restricted.

## Refunds

New Pro subscriptions can be refunded in full within 14 days of the first payment, no questions asked. After that, we do not refund partial months. Annual plans cancelled after the 14-day window remain active until the end of the paid year.

## Changing or cancelling a plan

Plan changes take effect immediately when upgrading and at the end of the billing period when downgrading. You can cancel at any time from the billing page. Cancelling does not delete your data; projects stay available in read-only mode for 60 days.

## Usage limits

Each plan includes a monthly allowance of storage and API calls. If you exceed the allowance we send an email warning at 80 percent and again at 100 percent. Overage is billed at the end of the month, and Enterprise customers can negotiate a fixed ceiling instead.

## Tax and invoicing details

Prices exclude VAT and sales tax, which are added at checkout where applicable. Customers can add their tax ID and company address on the billing page and the details appear on all future invoices. Past invoices cannot be edited, but support can reissue them.
