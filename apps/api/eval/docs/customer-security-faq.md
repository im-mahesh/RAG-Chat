# Customer Security FAQ

Answers to common security questions from customers and prospects. For binding commitments, refer to the data retention and privacy policy and your contract.

## How do you protect my data?

We take security seriously and use industry-standard measures to protect customer information. Our security programme is reviewed regularly and we are audited by independent third parties. Customer data is protected by layered controls across our network, applications and people.

## Is my data encrypted?

Yes. We use strong, bank-grade encryption to protect your information whenever it is stored and whenever it travels across the internet. Our security team reviews our cryptography choices regularly and upgrades them as standards evolve.

## What happens if there is a security incident?

We have a documented incident response process and a dedicated team that practises it. If we ever learn that your data has been affected we will tell you promptly and explain what we are doing about it. We continuously improve our processes after every event.

## Do you have security certifications?

We hold an annual SOC 2 Type II report and are certified against ISO 27001. Reports are available under NDA from your account manager. We also run an external penetration test every year.

## Can I run my own security assessment?

Enterprise customers may request permission to run an assessment of their own instance. Please contact your account manager at least two weeks in advance so that we can agree scope and timing.

## How do you vet your employees?

Employees with access to customer environments go through background checks where the law allows, and sign confidentiality agreements. Access is limited to what each role needs and is regularly reviewed.

## Where can I report a vulnerability?

Send details to the security team through our responsible disclosure page. We acknowledge reports within two business days and keep you informed until the issue is resolved.
