# Expense and Travel Policy

This policy explains what employees can expense and how to get reimbursed. It applies to all full-time and part-time staff and to contractors whose agreements include expense coverage.

## Meals

Meals while travelling for work are reimbursed up to $75 per day in total. This covers breakfast, lunch and dinner. Alcohol is not reimbursable under any circumstances. When a manager hosts a team dinner, the host may expense it up to $60 per person, and the itemised receipt must list every attendee.

## Travel approval

Any trip expected to cost more than $1,500 needs written approval from a director before booking. Flights under six hours must be booked in economy class. Longer flights may use premium economy. Book through the company travel portal so that negotiated rates apply; bookings made elsewhere are reimbursed only up to the portal price.

## Receipts

Submit receipts within 30 days of the purchase date. Expenses submitted after that window are rejected unless finance grants an exception. Receipts are not required for individual purchases under $25, but the expense report must still include a short description. Photos of receipts are acceptable if every line item is readable.

## Mileage

When an employee uses a personal car for business, mileage is reimbursed at $0.67 per mile. Commuting between home and the usual office is never reimbursable. Record the start and end addresses and the distance for each trip, and attach a map screenshot for trips over 100 miles.

## Corporate cards

Employees who travel regularly may be issued a corporate card. Card statements must be reconciled by the fifth day of the following month. Personal purchases on the corporate card must be repaid within ten days, and repeated violations lead to the card being withdrawn.

## International travel

For trips abroad, employees receive a daily per diem set by destination instead of itemising meals. Foreign currency charges are converted at the bank rate on the date of the transaction. All international travellers must enrol in the company's travel insurance before departure and carry the emergency contact card.

## Reimbursement timeline

Approved expense reports are paid by direct deposit within ten business days. Reports with missing receipts or unclear business purposes are returned to the employee and the clock restarts when they are resubmitted.
