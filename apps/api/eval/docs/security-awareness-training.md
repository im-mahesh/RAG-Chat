# Security Awareness Training Programme

The company runs a security awareness programme for all employees and contractors. This page describes the course and how it is administered.

## Who must take the course

Every employee, contractor and intern must complete the course. New starters take it during onboarding and everyone repeats a shorter refresher every 12 months. Completion is tracked automatically and reminders go to the person and their manager.

## Course modules

The course has six modules: passwords and second factors, phishing, handling customer data, secure remote work, physical security and reporting incidents. Each module takes about ten minutes and ends with two or three questions. The module on reporting incidents explains who to contact if a device is lost.

## Phishing simulations

Simulated phishing emails are sent to employees throughout the year. People who report the email using the Report Phish button are recognised in the monthly security newsletter. People who click are shown a short explanation straight away instead of being named.

## Role-specific training

Engineers receive an extra module on secure coding, covering injection, secrets handling and dependency risks. Support staff receive an extra module on verifying a customer's identity before changing account details. Managers learn how to recognise and escalate insider risk.

## Tabletop exercises

Twice a year the security team runs a tabletop exercise in which a realistic breach scenario is talked through by representatives from engineering, support, legal and communications. The aim is to find gaps in the response plan, not to assess individuals.

## Metrics

The security team publishes completion rates, phishing report rates and click rates each quarter. Teams with low completion are contacted by the security team. These numbers are used to improve the course, not to rank employees.
