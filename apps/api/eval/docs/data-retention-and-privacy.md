# Data Retention and Privacy Policy

This policy describes how long we keep customer data, how it is protected and what customers can ask us to do with it.

## Retention schedule

Application logs are kept for 90 days. Audit logs, which record administrative actions, are kept for one year. Database backups are retained for 35 days and then overwritten. Product analytics events are retained for 13 months. After these periods data is permanently deleted from primary storage.

## Deletion requests

Customers and their end users can ask for their personal data to be deleted. We verify the identity of the requester first, then complete the deletion within 30 days. Data in backups is not restored or altered; it expires naturally under the backup schedule. We confirm completion in writing.

## Encryption

All customer data is encrypted at rest with AES-256 and in transit with TLS 1.3. Encryption keys are managed in a dedicated key management service and are rotated every 12 months. Access to key material is limited to the security team and is logged.

## Access control

Employees receive the minimum access needed for their role. Access reviews take place every quarter and unused accounts are removed. Access to production customer data additionally requires an approved ticket that states the business reason and expires after 24 hours.

## Subprocessors

We use a small number of subprocessors for hosting, email delivery and error monitoring. The current list is published on our trust page. We give customers 30 days notice before adding a new subprocessor so they can object.

## Breach notification

If we confirm a personal data breach we notify the relevant supervisory authority within 72 hours of becoming aware of it. Affected customers are told without undue delay and receive a description of what happened, what data was involved and the steps we are taking.

## Data residency

Customers on the EU plan have their data stored in the Frankfurt region, eu-central-1, and it does not leave the European Economic Area. Support engineers outside the EEA cannot access EU customer data unless the customer grants temporary access.
