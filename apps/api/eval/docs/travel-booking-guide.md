# Travel Booking Guide

This guide explains how to book business travel through the company travel portal. The reimbursement rules themselves are in the separate expense policy.

## Searching and booking

Sign in to the travel portal with single sign-on, enter your route and dates and choose from the fares shown. The portal highlights fares within policy and warns you when a choice is outside it. Book as early as you can; fares for trips booked less than seven days ahead are usually much higher.

## Approval requests

Trips that need approval are sent to your approver from the portal. Approvers are asked to respond within 2 business days. If your approver is away, the portal routes the request to their delegate. Tickets cannot be issued until the request is approved, so plan for the waiting time.

## Hotels

The portal lists hotels with negotiated rates. The standard nightly limit is $220 per night in most cities and $320 per night in a small list of high-cost cities such as New York and San Francisco. Choose a hotel within a reasonable distance of the work location and prefer ones with free cancellation.

## Changing or cancelling a booking

Change or cancel in the portal rather than directly with the airline or hotel, so that credits are tracked in your name. Unused airline credits are valid for twelve months and must be used on business travel. Report cancelled trips promptly so the budget owner can release the funds.

## Car rental

Rent the smallest car that fits the group. Decline the rental company's extra insurance because the corporate policy already covers collision damage. Refuel before returning the car; refuelling charges added by the rental company are not reimbursed.

## Travelling with others

When several colleagues travel together, book them in one itinerary so the group can be changed as a unit. Each traveller is still responsible for their own expense report.

## Trouble on the road

If your flight is cancelled or your luggage is lost, call the travel assistance line printed on the emergency contact card. They can rebook flights and arrange hotels at any hour. Keep every receipt for costs you pay yourself.
