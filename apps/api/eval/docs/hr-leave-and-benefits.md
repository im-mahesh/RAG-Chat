# Leave and Benefits Guide

A summary of leave entitlements and benefit rules for all employees. Onboarding questions for new starters are answered in the onboarding handbook.

## Parental leave

Birth parents receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave and non-birth parents receive 12 weeks. Leave can be taken in one block or split into two blocks within the first year. Tell your manager and the people team at least eight weeks before the expected start date where possible.

## Sick leave

Employees have 10 paid sick days per calendar year. A doctor's note is required only when an absence lasts more than three consecutive days. Unused sick days do not roll over and are not paid out when you leave the company.

## Open enrolment

Benefit elections can normally be changed once a year during open enrolment in November. Changes take effect on 1 January. Outside that period elections can only be changed after a qualifying life event such as marriage or the birth of a child.

## Adding dependants

After a qualifying life event, dependants must be added within 30 days of the event. Proof such as a marriage certificate or birth record is needed. Coverage starts on the date of the event if the request is made on time.

## Retirement savings

The company matches pension contributions dollar for dollar up to 4 percent of salary. Contributions can be changed at any time in the benefits portal and take effect from the next payroll run. Matching contributions vest after one year of service.

## Bereavement and compassionate leave

Employees receive five paid days of leave on the death of an immediate family member and three paid days for other close relatives. Managers are expected to be flexible about additional unpaid time.

## Sabbaticals

After five years of service employees may apply for a four-week paid sabbatical. Applications need manager approval and are reviewed by the people team against project schedules.
